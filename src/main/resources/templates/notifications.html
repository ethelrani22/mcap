Latest Announcements

Stay updated with the latest admission notices and important announcements.

IMPORTANT First Round Admission Through CUET

Due to technical issues encountered during the payment process, the payment deadline for applicants provisionally allotted seats in the First Round of CUET admissions has been extended until 01/08/2026. All concerned applicants are advised to complete the payment at the earliest and no later than the extended deadline to confirm their admission. Failure to make the payment within the stipulated period will result in the automatic cancellation of the provisionally allotted seat.

NEW First Round Admission Through CUET

The First Round for admission through CUET has been completed, and seats have been provisionally allotted to eligible applicants. Allotted applicants must pay the admission fee between 24/07/2026 and 27/07/2026; failure to do so within the stipulated period will result in automatic cancellation of the seat, which will then be offered to eligible applicants in the next round of merit allotment.

NEW Slide-Up Option

Applicants opting for the Slide-Up process must pay a ₹1,000 Slide-Up Fee. If a higher-preference seat is allotted and accepted in a subsequent round, the ₹1,000 Slide-Up Fee will be adjusted against the admission fee. If no higher-preference seat is allotted, the applicant will retain the current allotted seat and must pay the remaining admission fee within the prescribed timeline to confirm admission.

NEW Admission Fee Mismatch

Due to a technical issue, some applicants may have paid an incorrect admission fee. Applicants who have paid excess or less than the required admission fee are advised to contact their respective institute after joining the classes for verification and further processing.

Portal Update (21/07/2026)

As 20/07/2026 was a holiday for the institutes, the application verification process is still in progress. The list of provisionally selected candidates will be generated and published on the portal once all institutes complete the verification process. After the list is published, selected applicants will be given 3 days to complete the admission/payment process. Applicants are advised to regularly check the portal for further updates.

Tura Govt. College invites Non-CUET applicants for FYUP (NEP) B.A. 1st Semester admission (2026 27) to appear for the Entrance Test on 23rd July 2026 (11:00 AM 12:00 Noon). Candidates must carry their registration form and required documents for verification. View Notice

Important Notice: Last Date for Application Submission: 10th July

Registered applicants who have not completed the payment and final submission of their application are advised to do so by 10th July. Only applications submitted on or before this date will be eligible for all regular rounds of seat allotment.

Application Portal Schedule Updated

The application portal will not be closed on 10th July and will remain open from 11th July onwards. However, only applications submitted on or before 10th July will be considered for all regular rounds of seat allotment.

Applications After 10th July

Applications received after 10th July will not be included in the regular seat allotment rounds. They will only be considered after all regular rounds are completed, subject to the availability of vacant seats.
